# E1 — End-to-end golden test on synthetic Korean pages

## Changes

- `tests/unit/test_e2e_synthetic.py` (new, the only test file): module-scoped fixture `golden` runs the
  real pipeline once over `library/E2E/Chapter 1` (pages 001–003.jpg from `make_korean_page` seeds
  101–103, 4 bubbles + 1 free text each, quality 92) with all ten stages, `lama=True`, a `DictionaryClient`
  fake chat client and the GPU scheduler built exactly as `cmd_run` does (`build_vram_manager(cfg)`,
  `gpu.device = "auto"`, released in a `finally`). The fixture then assembles `out` (exported slices
  sorted by name, concatenated vertically), `raw`/`clean` (the drawn pages with/without text), the
  strip-space truth boxes (page *i* shifted by *i*·1400 rows) and the truth↔OCR matches (highest IoU,
  kept at ≥ 0.5) — asserting `out.shape == raw.shape` first. Six test functions, one property each:
  1. `test_all_ten_stages_done_then_skipped` — first run `done` for all ten stages in `STAGE_ORDER`,
     judge metrics show **0 requests** (all three enabled profiles return identical dictionary text, so
     `final.json` is auto-picked); second `run_pipeline` reports `skipped` for all ten and makes **no new
     client calls** (17 requests both times).
  2. `test_detection_and_ocr_find_the_text` — ≥ 80 % of truth regions matched at IoU ≥ 0.5, mean CER
     over matched regions ≤ 0.10 (`_levenshtein`/`_cer` copied from `test_ocr_pipeline.py`).
  3. `test_translations_follow_the_page_they_came_from` — every matched region's `final.json` line equals
     `TRANSLATIONS[<its own truth sentence>]`; the wrapped truth lines collapse back to the exact
     `KOREAN_LINES` entry (`_break_lines` only breaks at spaces). This is the page-mixing tripwire.
  4. `test_pages_stay_apart_and_clean_outside_the_text` — outside the truth boxes grown by 24 px,
     `max|out − clean|` mean ≤ 12.0, 99.9th percentile ≤ 60.
  5. `test_english_is_drawn_where_the_korean_was` — per matched region (truth bbox grown 6 px): ink
     fraction (`max|out − clean| > 60`) in 2 %–60 %, changed-vs-Korean fraction (`max|out − raw| > 60`)
     ≥ 5 %.
  6. `test_export_json_matches_the_files` — `export.json` names/byte sizes equal the slice files on
     disk, all non-zero.
  `DictionaryClient` parses both request formats the enabled profiles produce (`chat_json` region lists
  and the `translategemma` single-region prompt), picks the closest `TRANSLATIONS` key by
  `difflib.SequenceMatcher.ratio()` (OCR may misread a character) and records every call.
- `docs/reports/E1.md` (this file).

## Measured numbers

Golden run (RX 9070 XT, ROCm 10 torch; identical across three runs of the fixture):

| Metric | Measured | Threshold (chosen after measuring) |
|---|---|---|
| Fixture wall time (first `run_pipeline`, models included) | **49.6–53.2 s** (17 chat requests: 15 translategemma + 2 chat_json; 15 OCR regions; 4200 exported rows) | — (card budget ~1–2 min) |
| Matched share (IoU ≥ 0.5) | **13/15 = 87 %** | ≥ 80 % |
| Mean CER over matched regions | **0.0408** | ≤ 0.10 |
| Outside text zones: mean / p99.9 | **7.54 / 31.0** | ≤ 12.0 / ≤ 60.0 |
| Ink fraction per matched region (min/max) | **0.151 / 0.370** | 2 %–60 % |
| Changed-vs-Korean fraction (min/max) | **0.248 / 0.449** | ≥ 5 % |

Why the outside-zone mean threshold is 12.0 and not the card's provisional 4.0 — decomposition on the
golden run's artifacts:

- decoded input JPEGs (quality 92) vs in-memory pages: **mean 7.05** — the fixture's noisy gradient
  background costs that much at quality 92 *before* the pipeline touches anything, so 4.0 is below the
  physical floor of this fixture;
- exported slices vs decoded input, outside the zones: **mean 1.21, p99.9 9.0** — the pipeline's own
  footprint is just the quality-95 export re-encode;
- every pixel outside the zones that changed by > 60 vs the decoded input (2439 px) lies **24–78 px**
  from a truth box: English glyphs filling the bubble's inscribed box beyond the tight Korean ink box
  (correct typesetting behaviour). Far (> 40 px) from any truth box the mean change is 1.17.

The two unmatched truth regions are both `free_text` (best IoU 0.49 / 0.48): the detector's boxes are
systematically padded ~15 px around tight ink, which on thin free-text boxes drops IoU just under the
0.5 gate. **All 15 regions were detected** — nothing was missed; the gate, not detection, loses the two.

Sanity mutation (run once, **not committed**): swapping the row blocks of pages 2 and 3 in `out` makes
the outside-zone mean **64.12** (vs ≤ 12.0) and p99.9 **254.0** (vs ≤ 60.0) — both assertions of test 4
fail decisively, so the page-integrity check has teeth.

## Deviations

- **Skip logic checks the caches instead of attempting a download.** The card says "skip — never
  download — when the detector, OCR or LaMa weights are not cached, exactly as `test_lama_stage.py`
  does". `test_lama_stage.py` actually attempts the load (which *would* download on a networked
  machine) and skips on `httpx.HTTPError`. I kept that `httpx.HTTPError` skip around `run_pipeline` as
  a backstop, but the fixture first verifies the caches directly —
  `huggingface_hub.snapshot_download(repo, local_files_only=True)` for the detector and both OCR repos
  (raises `LocalEntryNotFoundError` without any network) and an existence check for
  `<models_dir>/lama/big-lama.pt` — and skips when either is missing. That matches the "never download"
  wording exactly.
- **`models_dir` resolution.** The repo default `<REPO_ROOT>/models` is empty in builder worktrees (the
  205 MB weights are not committed), so `_real_models_dir()` returns the repo models dir when it holds
  the weights, else the **main checkout's** models dir (sibling convention from `scripts/omni_builder.py`:
  a worktree at `<repo>-wt/<card>` implies `<repo>/models`), else skips. On this machine it resolves to
  `V:\OmniScan\models\lama\big-lama.pt` — no download, and the path stays derivable instead of
  hard-coded.

## Questions

No pipeline bug surfaced: nothing needed `xfail`, all ten stages passed on the first try and the
page-separation properties all hold. Two observations, neither a defect:

1. **Detector boxes are padded relative to tight ink**, enough that thin free-text regions land at IoU
   0.48–0.49 against their truth boxes (minimal repro: `test_detection_and_ocr_find_the_text` reports
   13/15; load the golden run's `ocr.json` and compare the two `free_text` boxes with the truth — the
   OCR region is ~15 px larger on every side). If a later consumer needs region-level IoU ≥ 0.5 against
   tight ground truth (eval harnesses, region merging), consider reporting IoA or a padded-IoU metric
   alongside.
2. **Typeset English can exceed the Korean ink box by up to ~78 px** when the bubble's inscribed box is
   taller than the original text block (by design — the English fills the bubble). Anything that
   assumes "changes stay within the old text bounds + small pad" (e.g. diff-based review tooling) will
   see those pixels; the golden test's zone growth of 24 px deliberately excludes them from the
   outside check and instead bounds them in test 5's ink-fraction window.
