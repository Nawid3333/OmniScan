## Goal
The next stages (OCR, inpainting, typesetting) need pages that look like a webtoon page with **Korean text in speech bubbles,
free-standing text and sound effects**, plus the *exact* truth about what was drawn: every line's box and string, the bubble
outlines, a pixel-exact text mask, and the same page **without** any text (the ideal inpainting result). This card builds that
generator (deterministic from a seed), a helper that turns the truth into a `RegionsArtifact`, and a script that writes a
demo chapter into the library. Fonts are the SIL-OFL files already committed in `fonts/` — never system fonts (tests must run
identically on Windows, Linux and macOS). CPU only, no GPU, no network.

## Definitions (exact)
- **Corpus** `KOREAN_LINES` (exactly these 12 strings, in this order; each is one sentence that may be broken into several lines):
  `"괜찮아요? 던전이 열렸어!"`, `"민준 형, 빨리 도망가요!"`, `"그럴 리가 없어..."`, `"저 녀석은 S급 헌터야."`,
  `"내가 널 지켜줄게."`, `"뭐라고?! 다시 말해 봐!"`, `"게이트가 닫히기 전에 나가야 해."`, `"오랜만이야, 성진아."`,
  `"지금 무슨 일이 일어난 거지?"`, `"조용히 해. 누가 오고 있어."`, `"이건 시작에 불과해."`, `"약속할게. 반드시 돌아올 거야."`
  SFX strings (kind `sfx`) come from `("쾅!", "두근두근", "쿵!", "슈웅")`.
- **Determinism:** all randomness comes from one `numpy.random.Generator(numpy.random.PCG64(seed))`; the same arguments give
  byte-identical `image`/`clean` arrays. Draw text with PIL (`ImageDraw`), no other RNG.
- **Layout:** the page is divided into rows. Regions are assigned to rows in the order bubbles, then free text, then SFX; a row
  holds one region or two side by side (chosen by the RNG; two only for bubbles/free text); rows are stacked top to bottom with
  equal vertical slots inside the page (at least 40 px margin to every page edge and between neighbours). Inside a slot the
  region's position/size are jittered by the RNG but never leave the slot. Therefore the reading order is simply row-major,
  left to right, and boxes of different regions never overlap (bubble boxes included).
- **Background** (drawn first, over the whole page): `flat` = one light colour; `gradient` = a linear gradient between two RNG
  colours plus Gaussian noise σ = 6 (clipped); `noise` = random blocks (8×8) of RNG colours. Then bubbles are drawn: shape is
  an ellipse or a rounded rectangle (RNG), fill white `(255,255,255)` (80 %) or dark `(24,24,32)` (20 %), 3 px black outline
  (`(255,255,255)` outline for dark bubbles); `bubble_polygon` samples the outline (ellipse: 48 points; rounded rectangle: the
  4 corners' arcs, at least 12 points). `clean` is the page at this moment (copy).
- **Text:** bubble text uses `text_color` black on white fill and white on dark fill, font size 26–34 px (RNG, integer), lines
  broken at spaces (greedy) so that every line's advance width (`font.getlength`) is at most 0.72 × the bubble's width for
  ellipses and 0.85 × for rounded rectangles; a single word longer than that stays on its own line (no hyphenation). Lines are
  centred horizontally and vertically inside the bubble, line spacing = 1.25 × font size. Free text: 26–34 px, white with a 3 px
  black stroke (PIL `stroke_width=3`, `stroke_fill=(0,0,0)`), directly on the background, centred in its slot, width limit 0.9 × slot
  width. SFX: 90–130 px, `text_color` a saturated RNG colour, white stroke 5 px, single line, centred in its slot.
- **Truth boxes:** for every line, render it alone on a transparent `RGBA` layer of the page size with the same font, position,
  fill and stroke, and take `layer.getchannel("A").point(lambda v: 255 if v > 0 else 0).getbbox()` as the line's `bbox`
  (half-open, like `BBox`). `text_mask` is `np.any(np.asarray(image) != np.asarray(clean), axis=-1)`.
  (A pixel where the text colour equals the background colour would not show up in the mask; the fills above make that impossible
  for bubbles; for free text/SFX the mask may miss such pixels and that is accepted.)
- **`fill` of free_text/sfx** = the per-channel mean of `clean` inside `bbox`, rounded to ints.
- **`to_regions_artifact(page)`:** one `Region` per `RegionTruth`, `id` `r0001…` in order, `slice_index=0`, `kind` copied,
  `bbox`, `bubble_bbox`, `polygon=list(bubble_polygon) or None`, `reading_order` = the running index, `lang="ko"`,
  `orientation="h"`, `lines=[OcrLine(bbox=..., text=..., score=1.0, engine="truth")]`, `text=region.text`, `confidence=1.0`,
  `text_color=region.text_color`.
- **Script:** writes `--pages` pages named `001.jpg`, `002.jpg`, … (JPEG quality 92) into `<library_root>/<series>/<chapter>/`;
  page *i* uses `make_korean_page(seed + i, n_bubbles=4, n_free=1, n_sfx=1 if i % 2 == 1 else 0)`; prints one line per page and the
  next-step hint like `make_demo_chapter.py`. It also writes `truth.json` next to the pages ONLY when `--truth-out PATH` is given
  (that file holds one `RegionsArtifact` JSON object per page in a list; nothing else is written).

## Acceptance tests (CPU only)
1. **Determinism:** same seed/args → equal `image` and `clean` arrays and equal truth; a different seed → a different image.
2. **Mask/clean consistency:** `text_mask` equals the pixel difference of `image` and `clean`; `clean` contains no text: for every
   region the number of mask pixels inside `bbox` is ≥ 50 and the mask is empty outside the union of all `bbox` expanded by 6 px.
3. **Geometry invariants** (parametrised over 12 seeds and the three backgrounds): every `bbox`/`bubble_bbox` lies inside the page;
   every line box lies inside its region `bbox`, `bbox` == union of its line boxes; a bubble's `bbox` lies inside its `bubble_bbox`
   with ≥ 8 px margin on every side; no two `bubble_bbox`/`bbox` of different regions overlap; every polygon has ≥ 12 points and
   all points lie inside `bubble_bbox` (± 1 px).
4. **Reading order:** the regions are sorted by (row, x) — assert that the y-centres are non-decreasing whenever the vertical
   overlap of two consecutive boxes is < 50 % and that x0 increases otherwise.
5. **Counts and kinds:** `n_bubbles=4, n_free=1, n_sfx=1` → 4 `bubble_text`, 1 `free_text`, 1 `sfx`; `n_*=0` → an empty region list and
   `image == clean`. Requesting more regions than fit (e.g. `n_bubbles=30` on 800×1400) raises `ValueError` with a message that
   names the numbers.
6. **Text content:** every non-SFX region's text, with line breaks removed, is one of `KOREAN_LINES` (whitespace-insensitive);
   SFX texts come from the SFX tuple; the line strings joined by a space equal the corpus sentence.
7. **Fit:** in bubble regions every line's ink width ≤ 0.9 × the bubble width (loose bound; the definition's 0.72/0.85 bound is on the
   advance width); dark-fill bubbles have `text_color == (255,255,255)` and `fill == (24,24,32)`; white ones `(0,0,0)` and `(255,255,255)`.
8. **Font check:** Hangul is really rendered — for every line, the ink count inside its bbox is ≥ 20 × the number of non-space characters
   of the line... (if a glyph were missing the font would draw .notdef boxes: additionally assert that the rendering of `"가"` with
   `NanumGothic-Regular.ttf` differs from the rendering of `"\U0010fffd"`). `font_path("nope.ttf")` raises the documented
   `FileNotFoundError`. `font="Gaegu-Regular.ttf"` and `"NanumGothic-Bold.ttf"` also work.
9. **`to_regions_artifact`:** validates as `RegionsArtifact` (round-trip through `model_dump_json` / `model_validate_json`), ids
   `r0001…`, `lang == "ko"`, texts equal the truth, `lines[*].engine == "truth"`, reading orders are `0..n-1`.
10. **Script:** with `--library-root <tmp>` and `--pages 3` it writes `001.jpg 002.jpg 003.jpg` (natural order) of size 800×1400; the
    JPEGs decode to arrays within a mean absolute difference < 5 of `make_korean_page(seed + i).image`; `--truth-out` writes a list of 3
    valid `RegionsArtifact` objects; without it, no other file is created. Run it via `subprocess`/`runpy` with a tmp library root only —
    never write into the real library.
11. **Pipeline smoke:** in a tmp config (see `tests/unit/test_stages_ingest_slice.py` for the pattern) run `IngestStage` on the generated
    chapter: `ingest.json` loads, `strip_height` equals the sum of the page heights and `strip_width == 800`.
12. The whole suite, ruff and pyright are green.
